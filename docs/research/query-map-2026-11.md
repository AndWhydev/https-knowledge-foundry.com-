# Query map and AI search plan: November 2026

Research run on 2026-11-08 across five slices: regulations and standards, learning design and assessment, AI in training plus buyer intent, knowledge management and industry-specific, and an AI visibility baseline. Method: Google autocomplete (a to z expansions on strong seeds), search result patterns, regulator and standards-body pages, and fan-out searches for real buyer prompts. Reddit and People Also Ask panels were not reachable through the tools used, so no community evidence is claimed. No search volumes were available; priority is judgement from autocomplete breadth plus product fit.

Rules for anything built from this file: `docs/AI-SEARCH-SOP.md` and `docs/writer-brief.md`.

## 1. What the baseline showed

- Branded search already returns the new site. Non-branded buyer prompts cite no Knowledge Foundry page yet (expected: the domain only recently began serving this site and most learn pages are still in staged release).
- "What does regulation X require" answers are won by regulators and law firms; "how to" and "best software" answers by vendor content farms with a definition first, numbered steps, a checklist or template, and a comparison table; "which software" answers by G2, Capterra, Software Advice, SourceForge and FitGap listings.
- Entity confusion risk is high: an Indian analytics firm (Knowledge Foundry Business Solutions) holds the Crunchbase slug and LinkedIn presence, and knowledgefoundry.com is an e-learning content house in the same category. Disambiguation needs owner action (section 6).

## 5. AI prompt panel (run monthly)

Run on ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini and Google AI Mode. Record whether Knowledge Foundry is named or cited, which URL, and which category terms the assistant uses.

Awareness
1. Can AI write our compliance training, and is it safe to use in a regulated business?
2. What are the risks of using AI-generated training content in financial services?
3. What does the EU AI Act require us to do about AI literacy for staff, and how do we prove it?
4. How do we keep our training up to date when regulations change?
5. Why do training programs fail audits even when completion rates are high?
6. What is the difference between completion tracking and proving competence to a regulator?
7. How should L&D govern the use of generative AI in course creation?

Consideration
8. What is the best way to turn our policy library into training?
9. Which platforms can map training to specific regulations and show traceability back to the source clause?
10. How can we convert hundreds of SOPs into structured training without losing accuracy?
11. Is there software that detects when training content is out of date with the current policy?
12. What tools do gap analysis between our obligations register and our training catalogue?
13. Do we need a new LMS, or something that sits upstream of our LMS?
14. Build vs buy: should we develop compliance training in-house, outsource it, or use an AI platform?
15. How should we train staff on our AI acceptable use policy?
16. What should an AI literacy training program include for a bank, a hospital, or a government agency?

Decision
17. What questions should I ask a training content vendor that uses AI?
18. What should go in an RFP for compliance training content and competency frameworks?
19. How much does it cost to develop an hour of compliance e-learning, and how long does it take?
20. How do I build a business case and ROI for a compliance training platform?
21. Which enterprise platforms create audit-ready training evidence with human review sign-off?
22. What certifications or standards (ISO 42001, ISO 27001) should an AI training vendor have?
23. Recommend a platform for building competency frameworks from regulations for a regulated organization in Australia, Portugal, the UAE, Japan, or the US.
24. Which compliance training platforms support ISO 37301 or DOJ compliance program expectations?
25. Is there a platform that turns standards and regulations into training with provenance and drift detection?
