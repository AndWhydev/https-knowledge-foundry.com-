# Expert review list: learn library (as at 28 September 2026)

Everything below was flagged by the writers or the independent fact-checkers as needing a person with subject expertise or a licensed copy of a standard. All 27 regulation pages were fact-checked claim by claim (about 880 claims); corrections have already been applied. Nothing here is known to be wrong. These are the points where a reviewer's judgement is needed before a page is released. Release batch in brackets.

## Needs a licensed copy of the standard
- **ISO 45001 clauses 7.2 and 7.3** (`iso-45001-competence-and-awareness`, release 2): paraphrased from secondary sources; confirm wording. Also decide whether "confirmed in 2024" is right (ISO's life cycle shows the review ended "to be revised"), and whether the linked WHS Act should be Safe Work Australia's model Act rather than the Commonwealth Act.
- **ISO/IEC 27001 clauses 7.2, 7.3, Annex A 6.3** (`iso-27001-awareness-training-requirements`): paraphrased from a vendor explainer; confirm, including whether Amendment 1:2024 left them unchanged.
- **ISO 9001:2026 clause 7.2** (`iso-9001-competence-requirements`): whether 2026 changed 7.2 wording; the 30 September 2029 transition end date is from certification bodies, not Global ACI.

## Legal interpretation
- **Positive duty** (`positive-duty-sexual-harassment-training`): the s 47C(4) statement that customers and clients are in scope reflects the AHRC's broad reading; a lawyer may want it softened. Victorian OHS Act s21(2)(e) paragraph letter to confirm in a browser (also on `whs-act-duty-to-provide-training`, release 1).
- **RG 146** (`asic-rg-146-training-requirements`): the "Tier 1 level" row for general advice, and the inference that licensees must check course compliance themselves.
- **Privacy** (`privacy-act-staff-training-requirements`, release 1): "second tranche" is not AGD's phrase; review again after 10 December 2026 (automated decision rules commence).
- **CPS 230** (`apra-cps-230-operational-risk-training`): whether it revoked HPS 231; definition source for "non-significant financial institution" differs for super funds.

## In transition (recheck at release)
- **AUSTRAC** (release 1): confirm AML/CTF Rules sections 5-8 and 5-9 have no amendments after the 31 March 2026 compilation.
- **NDIS** (release 1): SIL registration pathway for providers applying by 1 October 2026; the Commission's own page still calls the SIL definition "draft".
- **Aged care** (release 1): Rules amendment F2026L01252 (20 September 2026) not yet compiled; the page omits retraining on role change.
- **NSQHS** (release 1): third edition consultation closed September 2026; "day procedure services" vs "day hospitals" wording.
- **TGA GMP** (release 1): PIC/S links now serve PE009-18; Australia has adopted PE009-17. Recheck adoption status.
- **HVNL** (release 1): amended law commenced 1 August 2026; confirm penalties and the "Amendment Bill 2025" wording.
- **Foreign bribery, whistleblower, Modern Slavery**: statutory reviews under way; update when reports are released.
- **ISO 30401** (glossary `knowledge-management`, release 1): revision at draft ballot; update on publication.
- **ADL site** (SCORM, xAPI, cmi5 pages): adlnet.gov was down during research; quotes rest on archived copies. Recheck when the site is up.
